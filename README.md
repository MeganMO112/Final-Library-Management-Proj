Algorithms implemented:
      1. Bubble sort: works by continuously comparing pairs of numbers (ex: 1st and 2nd, 2nd and third) and swapping so that the largest elements is the last in the set. Has an average tiem complexity of O(n^2) and does not perform well with large sets of data > worst performing of the 3 chosen 
      2. Binary Search Tree: works by essentially dividing the data in two halfs and sorting it, with data less than the root node going to the left and larger going to the right. Has an average time complexity of O(logn) and performs well with large data sets > best performing of the 3 chosen 
      3. Linear Search: simply searches through the data line by line until it finds the desired data. Has a time complexity of O(n) > middle in terms of performance
